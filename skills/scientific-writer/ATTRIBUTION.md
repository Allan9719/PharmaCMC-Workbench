# Attribution — Scientific Writer Module

This skill combines and adapts materials from two sources.

## Writing Quality Audit (5-Pass Review)

The 5-pass writing review workflow is adapted from the SciWrite project:

- **SciWrite fork:** https://github.com/Allan9719/sci_write
- **Original SciWrite project:** https://github.com/labarba/sciwrite
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

**Changes made:** Merged section-level writing models with the 5-pass prose-review workflow. Added WRITE / REVIEW / DIAGNOSE / LEARN operational modes. Created local compact reference files for manuscript-section scaffolding and writing-quality checks.

**Redistribution requirement:** Provide attribution to the original SciWrite project, link to the CC BY 4.0 license, and state that changes were made.

## Section Structure Models

The section models and Narrative Wrap workflow are inspired by:

> Hilary Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Native and Non-Native Speakers of English*, 2nd edition, 2020.

These materials are summarized as operational writing guidance. **The private extraction notes (`extracted-models/`) are excluded from public release.** Do not redistribute the source book or long source-derived excerpts unless you hold the appropriate rights.
