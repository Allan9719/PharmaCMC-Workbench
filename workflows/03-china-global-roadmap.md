# Workflow 03: China → Global CMC Roadmap

> **Modules:** CMC Research → CMC Forge (ASSESS + PLAN)
> **Typical duration:** 60–120 minutes (may span sessions)
> **Output level:** L2–L3

---

## When to Use

- China-first biotech planning international expansion
- Global pharma adapting products for China market
- Dual-filing strategy (China + US/EU simultaneous)
- Inbound licensing: overseas product → China manufacturing
- Outbound licensing: China product → global markets
- Post-approval change strategy across regions

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Define Company Posture and Strategy

Load: `skills/cmc-forge/references/china-global-cmc-operating-model.md`

Identify the company posture:

| Posture | Description | Key Challenge |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| China-only | Domestic market focus | Aligning with NMPA/CDE requirements |
| China-first then global | Start in China, expand outward | Building globally-compliant CMC foundation early |
| Global-first with China bridge | Already filed globally, adding China | Localization gap assessment |
| Inbound localization | Overseas product, manufacture in China | Tech transfer + comparability + local compliance |
| Outbound licensing | China product, license to global partner | Bridging studies, FDA/EMA-format documentation |

### Step 2: Regulatory Landscape Mapping

Load CMC Research references based on target regions:

| Region | Load From CMC Research |
|--------|----------------------|
| China (NMPA/CDE) | `china_cmc_special/regulatory_framework.md`, `declaration_practice/ind_cmc_zh.md` or `nda_cmc_zh.md` |
| US (FDA) | `basic_theory/ich_framework.md` (ICH M4Q), `cross_category/cmc_difference.md` |
| EU (EMA) | `basic_theory/ich_framework.md`, `cross_category/cmc_difference.md` |
| Cross-regional comparison | `cross_category/cmc_difference.md` (differences section) |

Also load: `skills/cmc-forge/references/regulatory-anchors.md` for ICH/FDA/NMPA guidance links.

### Step 3: Gap Assessment

Invoke **CMC Forge ASSESS mode** to evaluate current CMC readiness against multi-regional requirements.

**Assessment dimensions:**
1. **Regulatory dossier alignment** — CTD format, content depth, regional differences
2. **Quality system equivalence** — GMP compliance across jurisdictions
3. **Analytical method portability** — Method validation, transfer, ICH Q2(R2)/Q14 alignment
4. **Stability strategy** — ICH Q1A(R2) compliance, climatic zones, in-use stability
5. **Process validation** — FDA/EMA/NMPA expectations, PPQ requirements
6. **Supply chain** — Supplier qualification across regions, raw material controls
7. **Regulatory intelligence** — CDE review trends, FDA deficiency patterns, EMA requirements

**Load:** `skills/cmc-forge/playbooks/compliance-continuum.md` for lifecycle compliance assessment.

### Step 4: Evidence Roadmap Planning

Invoke **CMC Forge PLAN mode** to create the phased evidence roadmap.

**Planning framework:**
```
Phase 1 (Foundation):
- Build globally-compliant CMC narrative
- Establish core analytical methods to ICH Q2(R2)/Q14
- Complete process characterization

Phase 2 (China Filing):
- NMPA/CDE-specific requirements
- Chinese Pharmacopoeia 2025 alignment
- DMF filing strategy (if applicable)

Phase 3 (Global Bridge):
- Regional delta analysis (NMPA → FDA/EMA gaps)
- Bridging study design (analytical + non-clinical)
- FDA/EMA-format Module 3 adaptation

Phase 4 (Post-Approval):
- Cross-regional change management (ICH Q12)
- PACMP/Established Condition framework
- Ongoing comparability maintenance
```

**Load:** `skills/cmc-forge/templates/stage-gate-readiness-template.md` (China/Global readiness lens)

### Step 5: Deliver

Output structure:
```
⚠️ DRAFT — For controlled review. Not a GMP-controlled document.
Module: CMC Forge (ASSESS + PLAN)
Classification: L2–L3
Human review required: Yes — CMC Lead + RA (China + Global)

[China → Global CMC Roadmap]
- Executive summary
- Company posture and strategic context
- Regulatory landscape comparison
- Gap assessment (dimension-by-dimension scoring)
- Phased evidence roadmap with milestones
- Resource and timeline estimates
- Key risks and mitigation strategies
- Open questions for leadership decision
```

## Quality Checkpoints

- [ ] Company posture correctly identified and applied
- [ ] Regional requirements grounded in CMC Research sources
- [ ] Gap assessment has evidence basis, not speculation
- [ ] Timeline and resource estimates are realistic
- [ ] ICH Q12/Q14/Q2(R2)/Q5A(R2) considered where applicable
- [ ] No fabricated regulatory conclusions
- [ ] Bilingual terminology consistent (Chinese + English)
